# Ticket: Decide whether ADR-0008, ADR-0010, and ADR-0012 are restated before they are accepted

## What to build

A decision for the maintainer. Since 2026-10-21 ADR-0012 has been revised six times, ADR-0010
three times, and ADR-0008 twice, each revision adding what the work found. Each reads true
against the code (this revision corrected ADR-0012 where it no longer did), but they have grown:

- **ADR-0008** (about 3,000 words): two paragraphs of its *Context* are the evidence of the
  benchmark's runs, run numbers, CPU models, and Pearson coefficients, which a reader of the
  decision does not need and *Sources* could cite; its *Benchmark* bullet runs some thirty lines,
  the file name of a baseline, five refusals, the reference gate, and the median in one; ten of
  its twenty alternatives are about the benchmark; one alternative tells what the ADR first
  decided.
- **ADR-0012** (about 3,150 words): the *Mesh budget* bullet carries the argument for "at most
  sixteen split points per edge" inside the decision; *The rectangle* and *Mesh budget* together
  run some forty lines.
- **ADR-0010 and ADR-0012 share two clauses**: where the world stands relative to the query's
  origin (*The shell draws relative to the origin* and *Layers*), and what still keeps the
  rounding of far ends (a consequence in each). Each change to them took a revision of both
  (2026-10-23: the origin in ADR-0010, then the world in ADR-0012, then the split in both).

A normalization is not due and could not start: the record's mechanics start one only with no
proposed ADR and no ticket under `docs/plan/`, and fifteen ADRs are proposed and tickets are
open. Ways to curb the growth, for the maintainer to choose or reject:

- **A restating revision of each**, in its own pull request of ADRs only: the same decision in
  fewer words, the evidence moved to *Sources*, each shared clause kept in one of the two ADRs and
  cited from the other. No decision changes; the pull request lists each clause with where it now
  stands, as a normalization would.
- **Restate at acceptance**: the maintainer accepts each as it reads, and the next normalization
  restates them.
- **No change.**

The first is an agent's work once the maintainer asks for it; the clause list is what the
maintainer reviews.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given the options, when the maintainer decides, then the restating revisions are made as
      decided, or this ticket is deleted with the reason.
- [ ] Given a restating revision, when it lands, then every clause of the ADR before it stands in
      the ADR after it or in the one it now cites, as its pull request lists, and
      `scripts/check-docs.sh` passes.
